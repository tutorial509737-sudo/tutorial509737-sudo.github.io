# VOD Clipper - Terms of Service

Last updated: 19 November 2026

## 1. What VOD Clipper is

VOD Clipper is a personal, non-commercial desktop tool. It runs only on the computer of its developer and is not offered to the public as a hosted service. It downloads Twitch VODs that the developer is allowed to use, finds highlight moments, and renders short vertical clips with subtitles.

## 2. Use of the TikTok integration

The tool can upload a finished clip to the developer's own TikTok account using the TikTok Content Posting API. A clip is only uploaded after the user has reviewed it and has explicitly confirmed the post in a dialog that shows the preview, the caption, the chosen privacy level and the TikTok consent text. Nothing is posted automatically or on a schedule.
